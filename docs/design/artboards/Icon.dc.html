App icon

Four piano-roll notes in the four track colors, laid over a vermilion sun that doubles as a vinyl record. The sun is the app accent; the notes are the same colors and order as the track rows (piano, guitar, bass, drums), so the icon is also the key to the app. The ground is warm espresso so the colors glow in the Dock in both appearances.

Built on the macOS icon grid: 824 px body centered in a 1024 px canvas, superellipse corners, soft drop shadow, thin top sheen.
